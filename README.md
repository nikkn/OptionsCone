# OptionsCone

Probability, premium and positioning heatmaps for any stock with options.

OptionsCone turns the option chain into heatmaps on the price chart: touch and
expiry probabilities, implied volatility, open interest, spreads, time value
and premium relative to risk. It compares what the option market implies with
what the stock realized. For option traders and for anyone who holds stocks.

**Website and manual: https://nikolai-alexander.com/OptionsCone/**

![OptionsCone showing Broadcom (AVGO)](https://nikolai-alexander.com/OptionsCone/img/avgo/app.png)

## Free and Pro

The probability cone and the implied volatility, open interest and spread maps
are free. Pro adds the historical models and the seller maps: implied vs
realized, premium per unit risk and time value per day.
